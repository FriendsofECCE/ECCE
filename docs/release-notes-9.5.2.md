**ECCE 9.5.2.** Please report problems as [issues](https://github.com/FriendsofECCE/ECCE/issues).

**Platforms.** Linux (Debian 13; Ubuntu 24.04, RHEL/Rocky 9 and Fedora 44 packages are built on those systems but not tested). Windows 10 and 11. macOS, Apple silicon and Intel: experimental.

## Changes
- **Diffraction** (Tools > Diffraction..., and a Diffraction entry for every calculation with a periodic structure). Calculated patterns: a powder pattern with the Kα1/Kα2 doublet for Cu, Mo and Co sources, on by default and with the wavelengths diffractometers record, so that it lines up with a measured one (#587); the fibre diffraction pattern of a helix such as B-DNA or an α-helix (#971); single-crystal reciprocal-lattice layers, drawn on one crystal-wide intensity scale so that a nearly absent layer looks weak (#972, #984); precession photographs with exposure and colour scheme and click measuring; and the detector frame of a rotating crystal, with φ, oscillation, distance, source and noise settings and hkl and resolution-ring overlays (#985). **Experimental...** loads a measured pattern from text columns, PANalytical .xrdml or Bruker .brml; a file that cannot be read says why. The General Chemistry examples have a Diffraction topic (NaCl, KCl, quartz, B-DNA, α-helix).
- **CIF files:** the Builder opens and saves CIF (#358).
- **Docking with AutoDock Vina** (#455). A docking run type with the receptor and ligand prepared in ECCE as PDBQT, a docking box that is picked in the Builder and drawn with the pocket, and a Poses panel for the empirical score. **Edit > Select Protein** and **Select Ligand** replace the selection (#967, #1000); **Docking Box Around Selection** fills the ligand atoms from a selected ligand residue, and the run type dialog shows which ligand the automatic choice found, skipping water and single-atom ions (#962). Verify gives the docking advice as notes and does not count atoms in a deck that holds none (#962, #963). The Progress box shows the search bar and the best score (#1001). Save of a 4710-atom calculation and the Poses panel on a protein are much faster, and the unit cell of a crystal file is no longer applied to a docking calculation (#964, #966, #968).
- **QSAR:** **Tools > Batch Add from SMILES** makes one calculation per molecule of a table, with an option to replace calculations of the same name that have not been launched. **QSAR Table** collects descriptors from the calculations, and **QSAR Analysis** does PCA and PLS, with prediction for new molecules; `ecce-qsar` gives the same results on the command line (#961).
- **GROMACS umbrella sampling** (#762): a Pull Force panel, windows restrained at their own target distance, an explicit box size in Prepare, and a WHAM analysis with the PMF, the window histograms and an overlap warning. The page shows the plots and a short summary; the full text, with every extremum, overlaps and commands, is under Details... with its own Export .csv. `ecce-umbrella` does the same from the command line. The help follows a lab through Prepare to WHAM.
- **Show Hydrogen Bonds** uses the GROMACS geometric criterion (donor-acceptor distance 3.5 Å, angle 30°), N, O, F and S donors, N, O, F and halide acceptors, and the minimum image, with a settings dialog. The old cutoff was 1.41 Å (#978). Redrawing it while stepping through a trajectory is faster on large boxes (#982).
- **Builder and viewer:** transparent atoms (Atom opacity in the Display panel and Custom Style), and a clipping plane (x, y, z, Miller or three-atom plane, offset, flip) that cuts atoms, bonds, surfaces and the cell (#879). **Render > Display Settings...** has style, colours and labels in one place, with global surface colours and opacity (#881). **Add from SMILES** builds a molecule with its charges and stereochemistry from ECCE's own SMILES reader, without Open Babel, and **Replace Group** swaps a group for a fragment (#833, #867). **Distort Bonds** is modeless with Apply and Close, one Undo step per Apply (#914). The unit cell edges carry a/b/c labels (#723). Floating panels are ordinary windows and the viewer can be brought to the front (#895).
- **Properties:** Dipole Moment shows optional bond dipoles from a chosen set of atomic charges (#849). **Density of States** in the Builder gives TDOS, PDOS of two atom sets, OPDOS and integrated OPDOS, with the MO list under a clicked peak (#171). The Density Profile along a line gives electrons per length, optionally within a cylinder (#749). Thermochemistry shows the point group, symmetry number and rotor type used, warns near a higher point group, and takes a σ override in the panel and in `ecce-thermo` (#854). The Energy Calculator has a 1 M standard-state term on G and a log K line (#835).
- **Results file:** every finished calculation with orbitals gets a checked `results.h5` beside it, which also holds vibrations and the scan, optimisation, IRC and MD traces; the Density Map, Density Profile, `ecce-density-field` and MO composition read it (#690, #883).
- **Reaction rates and fragment analysis:** `ecce-rate` gives transition-state-theory rate constants with Wigner and Eckart tunnelling, shown in a Rate box in workflows and in the Reaction overview, which takes calculation names, draws an energy profile for each reaction with a transition state, and has a Details page with the treatment and its references (#872, #885, #900). `ecce-fragorb` expands the MOs of a complex in the orbitals of fragment calculations, with PDOS and OPDOS, and the MO Diagram can show the interaction diagram from fragment calculations (#201).
- **Workflows:** a play button on each box and an auto-run option for the remaining steps (#736). Several calculations of a folder can go into a new or existing workflow, with their links (#874). A 4-aminopyridine protonation workflow replaces the Na⁺ hydration one (#841), and the Na⁺(H₂O)₆ workflow has water (CPCM) steps.
- **Organizer:** the New menus group the codes by kind, and the Tools menu is grouped. A **Servers** button on the toolbar (#795), **Move to...** and **Copy to...** with a folder chooser, also between Local data, SSH folders and ECCE servers (#929, #890), and the tree and table follow changes on their own (#889).
- **Command line:** `ecce-rerun` makes a Ready copy of a killed, failed or finished calculation through the running Organizer, optionally launching it, and `--last-geometry` continues from the last stored geometry (#825, #837, #886). `ecce-mkdir` makes a folder (#870). `ecce-reconnect <calc> jobid=N` attaches a job already in the queue (#887). `ecce-copy` copies between trees (#890). `ecce-ensemble` picks members of a CREST ensemble and finds the lowest-G minimum (#836); `ecce-morse` fits a Morse curve (#858).
- **Quantum ESPRESSO:** relax and vc-relax store the energy of each optimisation step with its frame (#1003); pw.x outputs can be imported (#447); Duplicate with Final Orbitals starts from the charge density (#407); a Verify check catches pseudopotentials of different functionals (#945).
- **Other codes:** Duplicate with Final Orbitals also for MOPAC and CP2K (#407), MOPAC COSMO (#821), ORCA RI approximation None, RIJCOSX or RIJONX (#834), g and h shells in the orbital views of Gaussian 16, ORCA and NWChem (#861, #863), ORCA -3c composites import with their theory and basis (#898), relaxed scans of ORCA and Gaussian 16 keep the orbitals of every point (#549), and IRC points of Gaussian 16 and ORCA arrive while the job runs (#948, #949). ECCE-QM has the D3(BJ) dispersion correction (#453). CREST has a No topology check option and warns when the topology check removed every conformer (#831), and shows progress for quantum cluster growth (#862). The ML potential has site screening for periodic hosts (#728).
- **Examples:** General Chemistry has Packing in solids (simple cubic, bcc, fcc, hcp, NaCl, CsCl), the Quantum ESPRESSO crystals Al, Na and α-Po, Intermolecular interactions, and H₂ dissociation curves. The Structure Library is organised by chemistry (#405).
- **Removed:** DirDyVTST, Polyrate and Amica; calculations stored with them still list (#873).
- **Windows and macOS:** ECCE starts under a user profile with å, ä or ö (#922), the local broker waits for a slow first start (#958), `ecce.cmd` calls find.exe by its full path (#973), and a start blocked by Smart App Control says so and how to switch it off (#933). The macOS minimum follows the build, with an extra Intel package for macOS 10.13 (#923).

## Fixes
- The Builder can select atoms by clicking after View > Atom Labels with numbering, and a display-style change no longer zooms out to the whole system (#975, #977). Playing a GROMACS trace keeps a zoomed or panned view (#876). Boundary atoms are drawn after Replicate (#974).
- Terminate ends a program that outlives the queue's cancel and says whether anything is left in the run directory (#855). Job staging folders are removed (#924).
- Shift+click and Ctrl+click in the project table select as expected (#925); the Run submenu is back on a single row (#875); opening a folder with many calculations is faster (#928); the Organizer no longer crashes on a tree reload during the first-start example import on Windows (#932).
- The `ecce-*` request tools acknowledge on arrival, so a busy Organizer is not reported as not running (#897), and exit at once when it ends after acknowledging (#816). `ecce-thermo` and `ecce-rate` run outside the ecce wrapper (#938).
- Gaussian 16 orbitals and densities are correct for re-contracted basis sets such as cc-pVXZ (#921). Gaussian 16 traces reach `results.h5` (#946), and the IRC includes the TS structure (#947). ORCA orbital views work for basis sets with g functions (#861), and ORCA imports with no printed basis or a composite method keep their theory and basis (#898).
- A line of 254 or more characters no longer cuts a launched deck. A NWChem MD task after an optimisation starts from its .qrs file (#865). The NWChem dielectric constant has no unit (#848). Multiplicity 9 is Nonet (#853).
- The MO panel Graph and Graph by Symmetries are filled for runs without a symmetry column (#882). The MOs panel no longer widens the side column (#843). The MO Diagram is offered for MOPAC (#911).
- Workflow steps from recipes become Ready, launching from the workflow view does not select the step, and a CREST step after xTB is Ready (#901, #902, #904). A CREST protomer shows its proton (#912, #920), and a recipe's 6-31+G(d) finds its library basis (#920).
- Builder Replace Group works with the two atoms of a bond selected (#909). File > Export > Supporting Information works (#913). Switching from ELF to MOs clears the surface (#907).
- Units in labels read Å, Å², °, 2θ and cm⁻¹ (#998). Packages no longer carry `ecce-ecce-*` wrappers (#960).

## Known issues
- **Windows:** uninstalling takes long (#815). Local sessions still depend on `mosquitto.exe` (#990).
- **GROMACS, CP2K, Quantum ESPRESSO** and the **ML potential** are experimental. **The MO diagram** is experimental.

The bundled atomipy (1.1) and min.ff (commit d12b895b) are those of 9.5.0. The newest min.ff renames its parameter files, which ECCE does not follow yet.
